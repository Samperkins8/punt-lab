    Picking a race

    There's no free feed of racing prices, so compare the bookie's fixed price with Betfair yourself and enter both above.

    1. Australian metro meetings (Saturday Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane) or UK and Irish racing. These have the most money on Betfair. Skip Japan, France, Turkey, South Africa and most midweek overseas cards.
    2. Wait until 5–10 minutes before the jump. Betfair fills up late, so lay prices are tightest then and scratchings are already known.
    3. Pick a horse at about $4–$8 where the bookie's price is within about 5% of Betfair's lay price. Short favourites keep less of a bonus bet.
    4. Fixed-odds Win bet only, not tote, place or each-way, so your price is locked and matches the Betfair Win market.
    5. Check the amount available next to the lay price covers your lay stake. Enter it above and the calculator will warn you.
    6. Avoid maidens and races with a late scratching risk where you can. A scratching means deductions at the bookie and a reduction on Betfair, which don't always cancel out.

    How it works
    Bonus bet
    The bookie doesn't return the stake if it wins, so you only win the profit. Lay stake = stake × (price − 1) ÷ (lay price − commission). You end up with the same amount either way, usually 65–80% of the bonus bet's value.
    Normal bet
    Your own money, returned with the winnings. Used to qualify for a promo ("bet $50, get a $50 bonus bet"). Lay stake = stake × price ÷ (lay price − commission). You lose a little either way (the "qualifying loss"); the bonus bet you get then makes it back.
    Liability
    What the Betfair bet costs if the bookie bet wins. It must be in your Betfair account when you place the lay, and comes back when the game is settled.
    Bonus bets on longer prices
    A bonus bet keeps more of its value at longer prices ($3–$6), because the stake isn't returned. The table ranks today's games by this.
    Refund promos ("1 leg fails, get a bonus bet")
    These are worth taking on bets you were making anyway; the refund is a bonus bet you can then lock in here.
    Horses
    Works the same way: a fixed-odds Win bet at the bookie, laid in Betfair's Win market for the same race. Racing commission differs by market, so check it. If another runner is scratched after you bet, the bookie applies a deduction to your winnings and Betfair reduces your lay price by that runner's reduction factor; they're usually close but not identical, so betting near the jump is safest. If your own horse is scratched, the bonus bet is normally refunded and the Betfair lay is void.
    Before you start
    You need a Betfair Australia account. Lay at the price showing when you place the bookie bet, and place both bets one straight after the other so the prices don't move. Bookies can restrict accounts that only bet on promos.
