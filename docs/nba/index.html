How the multis are picked
Model
This season's games (the last 5 count 1.5× as much as older ones) blended with last season (last season counts as 8 games' worth, less if he played under 40 games), shifted towards games played in tonight's situation (a teammate in or out), adjusted for the opponent's defence against his position and last season's head-to-head (shrunk by how much of the opponent's roster is still the same, worked out from last season's teams, and by sample size), adjusted for home or away (from his own split, shrunk for small samples) and tonight's expected pace (the game total against the slate average), scaled by expected minutes (cut on the second night of a back-to-back), and blended 60/40 with the US betting market's line where one is available.
Est. chance all land
The legs' chances multiplied, adjusted for same-game links: two rebound legs from one game work against each other, two points legs from one team share the shots, other same-game legs move a little together with pace.
Est. Sportsbet price
Each leg's fair price less a typical bookie margin, multiplied together. Every multi must reach $2.00 on this estimate; check the real price in the app.
Profit ($1 each)
What $1 on that multi every day would have made, at the real price when every leg had one, otherwise the estimated price. * means some older days had no price saved and are left out.
Worth it if it pays at least
1 ÷ the chance all legs land. If Sportsbet's price for the whole multi is at or above it, the model says it's worth taking.
Rules
No leg priced under $1.20 (where no price is known, the line is stepped up until the model is about 83% or lower). Never two rebound legs from the same game. Players listed out or doubtful are left out.
Overrides
Edit overrides/nba.txt in the repo to skip a player, set minutes, mark someone out or enter Sportsbet odds. The next run uses it.
Caveat
Early-season samples are small and bookies price props well. This is analysis, not a guarantee.
