How the multis are picked
Model
This season's games (recent games count extra; the weekly tune sets how much) blended with last season (last season counts as 8 games' worth, less if he played under 40 games), shifted towards games played in tonight's situation (a teammate in or out), adjusted for the opponent's defence against his position and last season's head-to-head (shrunk by how much of the opponent's roster is still the same, worked out from last season's teams, and by sample size), adjusted for home or away (from his own split, shrunk for small samples) and tonight's expected pace (the game total against the slate average) and his team's share of that total (an underdog's scorers are marked down by the line, a favourite's up), scaled by expected minutes (cut on the second night of a back-to-back, for both teams when the line is 10 or more, and for the underdog from a line of 7, since its starters sit first when a game gets away), and blended 60/40 with the bookies' over/under line (margin removed) where one is available.
Est. chance all land
The legs' chances multiplied, adjusted for same-game links: two rebound legs from one game work against each other, two points legs from one team share the shots, a team to win works against legs from the team it's playing, other same-game legs move a little together with pace.
Bet on
Each multi is priced as a whole at TAB, Neds and Bet365, and shows the app whose prices for every leg multiply to the most, with the others for comparison. Multis are picked on that price, so a $2+ multi pays $2+ in that app. When no one app prices every leg yet, an estimate is shown instead (fair price less a typical margin); check it in the app.
Late checks
15–75 minutes before each game, its injury news is checked again. The morning's multis stay as they were (you may have bet them), but a leg whose player is ruled out is marked and its multis get a warning with a suggested swap. The game is re-analysed, so teammates who gain from a late out show updated chances (and new legs worth a look are added). Prices are refreshed only when someone's status changed.
Prices
Only TAB, Neds and Bet365. Each morning every game is priced at Neds (from OddsPapi), and the games the multis use most are also priced at TAB and Bet365 (from The Odds API, within its free daily credits).
Stake
Type the price on offer and the card suggests a share of your betting budget: a quarter of the Kelly amount ((chance × price − 1) ÷ (price − 1)), at most 3%, and nothing when the price is under break-even. Prices you type stay on your phone only.
Profit ($1 each)
What $1 on that multi every day would have made, at the real price when every leg had one, otherwise the estimated price. * means some older days had no price saved and are left out.
Worth it if it pays at least
1 ÷ the chance all legs land. If the app's price for the whole multi is at or above it, the model says it's worth taking.
Rules
No leg priced under $1.20 (where no price is known, the line is stepped up until the model is about 83% or lower). Never two rebound legs from the same game. At most two legs from one game in a 3- or 4-leg multi and three in a bigger one, so one bad game can't sink it (and apps price same-game legs lower than the legs multiplied). Never a team to win with a leg from the team it's playing. Players listed out or doubtful are left out.
Overrides
Edit overrides/nba.txt in the repo to skip a player, set minutes, mark someone out or enter odds. The next run uses it.
Caveat
Early-season samples are small and bookies price props well. This is analysis, not a guarantee.
